Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Overview

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Technical data: basic information such as session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To store your expenses and show you summaries and breakdowns.
  • To keep the service secure and working properly.
  • To contact you about important changes to your account or these policies.

4. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Passwords are never stored in plain text. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it when required by law, or when needed to protect the rights and safety of Spendly and its users.

6. Cookies

Spendly uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your session. We do not use cookies for advertising.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information and expense data will be removed, unless the law requires us to keep it.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • Access and update the information in your account.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Ask us any questions about how your data is handled.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Please also review our Terms & Conditions.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please get in touch with the Spendly team.
